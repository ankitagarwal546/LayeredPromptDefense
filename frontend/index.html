Limits of this study
  • Two small local models only; large hosted models may behave differently.
  • One human reviewer, who could see which preset produced each answer; clean documents were AI-written.
  • Two repeats at temperature 0 are near-identical, so the real sample is about 75 attacks.
  • "Unsure" answers are left out and shown as unresolved. Nothing is claimed as statistically significant.
The problem

Indirect prompt injection: the attacker never talks to the AI. They hide an order in content the AI reads later.

STEP 1

You ask for a summary
